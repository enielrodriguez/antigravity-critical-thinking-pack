# Formal Writing, Claims & Career Deliverables Workflow

Produce sharp, credible formal documents and career materials grounded strictly in confirmed facts, appropriate institutional register, and zero AI-sounding fluff.

---

## 1. Strict Factual & Anti-Inflation Guardrails
1. **Zero Fabrication or Metric Inflation**: Never invent percentages, revenue impact, team sizes, or credentials not provided by the user. If a bullet would be stronger with a real metric, insert a clear placeholder (e.g., `[X %]` or `[N users]`) and ask the user to fill it in—never guess.
2. **Opaque Proprietary Entities**: Treat undefined internal project names, corporate tools, or private acronyms strictly as opaque proper nouns. Expand an acronym only if it is verifiable public/industry standard knowledge; never deduce or hallucinate internal company terminology.
3. **Match Reported Scale**: Never upscale routine maintenance, support, or participation into "led enterprise-wide transformation" or "architected global strategy". Credibility beats inflated adjectives.
4. **Cut AI Clichés & Filler**: Eliminate empty corporate clichés (*"passionate self-starter"*, *"synergistic"*, *"delve into"*, *"testament to"*, *"I hope this email finds you well"*). Prefer concrete verbs, verifiable outcomes, and plain, precise prose.

---

## 2. Deliverable-Specific Protocols
- **CVs / Resumes & Cover Letters**:
  - Tailor strictly to the target role's confirmed requirements using only real experience from the user's background.
  - Prioritize clarity, scannability, and verifiable scope over buzzword density.
  - Flag genuine gaps honestly to the user instead of papering over them with vague claims.
- **Formal Complaints, Claims & Administrative Appeals**:
  - Structure chronologically with: 1) Identification & reference numbers, 2) Objective statement of facts (dates, amounts, evidence), 3) Applicable contract clause or legal basis (verify jurisdiction if needed), 4) Exact remedy requested and response deadline.
  - Maintain a firm, factual, unemotional tone—avoid threats or hyperbole that weaken legal/institutional leverage.
- **Professional / Institutional Emails**:
  - State the purpose or request in the first two sentences, list concrete facts/dates cleanly, and close with an unambiguous call to action.

---

## 3. Verification Before Delivery
1. Confirm zero invented facts, metrics, or expanded private acronyms slipped into the draft.
2. Confirm the language, tone, and formal conventions match the recipient's culture and jurisdiction.
3. Deliver the ready-to-send text cleanly, followed (only if needed) by a brief list of placeholders or factual gaps the user should verify.
